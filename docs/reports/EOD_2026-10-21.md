### 1.2 SSO local QA

The vendor sample was studied rather than copied. Three things in `sampleSignout.aspx.vb` are deliberately **not**
reproduced: the `Domain="mju.ac.th"` cookie (not ours; a parent-domain cookie is readable by every MJU
subdomain), the sample's own client ID, and an expiry with no `Path`/`Secure`/`HttpOnly`/`SameSite`.

**The important finding is a contract mismatch:** the sample shows the callback parameter is **`ac`**, and the
token exchange is `POST …/token.aspx` with `{clientID, code}` — no client secret, no `grant_type`, no `state`
echoed back. That is **not OAuth 2.0**. RAE V2 reads `code` and requires a secret, but the portal path is
hard-blocked with `SSO_NOT_READY`, which is the correct posture. **No protocol was changed on the strength of a
sample file.** This needs vendor confirmation.

| Prior redaction commits on `origin/main` | **NO** — `04ff9d0` and `850e3fc` (2026-10-16) are **not ancestors of `main`** |

| History remediated | **NO** |

**Why this is a finding and not a cosmetic issue:** a Thai national ID always passes the mod-11 checksum. A
committed literal that passes it is either a real person's ID or indistinguishable from one, so the repository
treats it as a leak either way. `AGENTS.md` names `****5351` explicitly as a value that must never be
reproduced — and it is still in public `main`.

**Why it survived an earlier remediation:** the 2026-10-16 redaction commits were authored on other branches and
never reached `main`. `docs/memory/PROJECT_STATE.md` records the file-level redaction (R2) as done. **That is
now demonstrably wrong for `main`.** This is the single most important correction in this closeout.

**Status: CONTAINED IN SOURCE (pending PR #60 merge) · NOT REMEDIATED IN HISTORY · NOT RESOLVED.**
This report does **not** claim the incident is resolved.
