# 2569 holiday evidence — research in progress

The MJU Personnel Division's [2026 holiday page](https://personnel.mju.ac.th/holiday69.php)
is dated 12 January 2026 and cites the annual government holiday announcement and the
cabinet's special holiday on 2 January. It cannot by itself confirm later announcements.
The [official annual announcement](https://ratchakitcha.soc.go.th/documents/17082311.pdf)
defines recurring holidays but does not give the 2026 date for every movable holiday.

Later government sources identify [13 May as Royal Ploughing Day](https://mot.go.th/PRNews/1470)
and [16 December as a special government holiday](https://www.thaigov.go.th/th/news/171072).
The [28–29 September special holiday](https://www.thaigov.go.th/th/news/169410) was limited
to Bangkok, Nonthaburi, Pathum Thani and Samut Prakan; it must not be applied to Chiang Mai
on that authority alone. MJU applicability and any university-specific later announcements
still need verification. In particular, an older official school calendar lists 11 May for
Royal Ploughing Day, whereas subsequent government sources list 13 May; use the later
confirmed date, not the older provisional calendar.

No calendar rows have been approved or inserted as part of this research. Calendar coverage,
scope, approval provenance, re-derivation and backup must be verified before production use.
